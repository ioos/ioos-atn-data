# Animal Telemetry Network Data Assembly Center Overview

The Integrated Ocean Observing System (IOOS) Animal Telemetry Network
Data Assembly Center (ATN DAC) is designed to serve as an access point
to search, discover and access animal telemetry data, and associated
oceanographic datasets, from a wide variety of species and platforms.
The concept behind this website was put forth in the [ATN White
Paper](https://cdn.ioos.noaa.gov/media/2017/12/ATN-Ioos-White-Paper-Final.pdf)
document, entitled, *Toward A U.S. Animal Telemetry Observing Network
(US ATN) For Our Oceans, Coasts and Great Lakes.*
